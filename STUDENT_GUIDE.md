# SIRA Mathematics Readiness Assessment

Instructor note: share for the agreed diagnostic session. The assessment is hosted in the cloud and does not require your computer to stay on. Send only the student name/access code; never share signing secrets or administrative credentials.

---

Your mathematics diagnostic helps us plan lessons around your strengths and the areas you would like to develop. It is not an official college placement examination.

**Website:** https://sira-mathematics-assessment.vercel.app  
**Student name:** enter your full name  
**Access code:** provided separately  
**Format:** 60 multiple-choice questions in 60 minutes, in English

Before starting, choose a quiet place, charge your device and check your internet connection. A laptop or tablet is useful; the assessment also works on a phone. Have paper and a pen for working. Work independently without a calculator, notes, search engines or AI unless your instructor approves an accommodation.

1. Enter your full name and access code. Your timer begins when you select Start.
2. Choose one answer per question. You can skip questions, return to earlier answers and flag questions to review.
3. Optionally select Sure, Unsure or Guessing. These ratings help your instructor understand your reasoning.
4. Keep one browser tab open. Your timer continues through refreshes and interruptions. Responses save locally and sync while online; only responses received by the server before the deadline can be graded.
5. When finished, select Submit assessment and confirm. At time expiry, editing ends automatically.
6. Wait for the submission confirmation. If delivery fails, use Retry submission and contact your instructor. Save a private response backup if offered; do not share it publicly.

Your detailed report goes to your instructor, Aymane Chellak, at aymane.chellak@outlook.fr. The results guide your next lessons. Being unsure is useful information; you are not expected to know every answer.
